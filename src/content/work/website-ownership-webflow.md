title: "Marketing owns the website"
status: published
order: 2
featured: false
client: "Appsilon"
summary: "Off an engineering-dependent stack and onto one the team runs itself — now mostly through AI."
problem: >-
  The site ran on WordPress + Gatsby, so every change went through engineering —
  a new landing page, a copy fix, a campaign page. The people closest to the
  message were the slowest to change it.
approach: >-
  The move to Webflow started as one of my team's ideas; I backed it and we ran
  the migration together. We rebuilt the services pages around what buyers
  actually weigh up (Shiny Dashboards, the R & Python platform, SCE, the GxP
  audit), rewrote the homepage around the buyer's journey, and stood up a
  Resources Hub as one home for the lead magnets. These days most changes run
  through AI — a Claude Code–powered setup wired to Webflow over MCP drafts and
  publishes posts, runs analyses, proofreads, and reviews — so we barely open
  the CMS for routine work.
result: >-
  The team owns the site end to end and ships far faster than in the old days —
  no engineering queue, no waiting on a ticket to change a headline. The win is
  ownership and speed.
