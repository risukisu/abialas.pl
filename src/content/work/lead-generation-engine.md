title: "The lead-generation engine"
status: published

featured: false
client: "Appsilon"
summary: "A repeatable engine to source qualified pipeline and stay in conversation with the market."
problem: >-
  Lead gen was pure inbound and unpredictable — no structured way to reach the
  market, capture intent, and feed sales a steady, qualified flow. Mostly we
  hoped the right people found us.
approach: >-
  I built an engine from parts that compound: lead magnets and ebooks (the
  Functional Programming ebook alone passed 1,000 downloads), a content and
  newsletter rhythm to stay in front of the market, and a webinar / online-event
  program that gets the right rooms together — up to ~400 registrations a
  webinar, around half from ICP companies. The first GxP Validation Summit
  Online pulled 100+ signups from the likes of Novartis, Merck, AstraZeneca,
  Moderna, Bayer, and Recursion. Underneath: HubSpot lead scoring and an allbound
  model, so we weren't betting everything on inbound.
result: >-
  A predictable way to source and qualify pipeline instead of waiting for it.
  Webinars drew ~50% ICP attendance, the magnets and events fed the strict-bar
  funnel, and outbound gave the team a channel it could actually steer.
