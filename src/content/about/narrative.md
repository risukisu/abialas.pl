I have a degree in environmental conservation and spent a decade working in NGOs before ending up running marketing for tech companies.

Most of what I know came from doing the work badly for a while and running on instinct. Experiments are fun!

I'm currently serving as head of Marketing at [Appsilon](https://appsilon.com), where I am leading an amazing team of talented people 💙, and also building and implementing marketing strategies for growth.

I run marketing like an engineering org: focused campaigns, systems to deliver them, and the work tied back to revenue with a sprinkle of [AI, but used in a smart way](https://risu.pl/blog/i-love-ai-i-hate-ai/).

Here, I write about the systems behind that work, plus shorter notes on AI and leadership when there's something worth saying.
