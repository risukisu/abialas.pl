The Title screen, three ways

The first screen of the game, before the Roster: a lone hero at a campfire under the night sky, the monsters waiting where the light ends, the title, and the menu. Three concepts, none chosen yet, and none of it is in the game. Each one is live: click it, walk the menu with the arrow keys, and Enter or a click says what a row opens.

Every concept carries the same menu, in the game's own words: Continue (shown once a character exists), New character, Roster, Settings and Quit (in the desktop app only), and the records every character adds to: the Compendium, the Graveyard, the Hall of Fame, and the Credits. Where those sit is what the three try: a panel and a row of icons, things in the world, or words in the dark.

Art exploration

The game still paints every tile in code while its first real pixel art is drawn. These are the looks that were tried, each one drawing the same test room. On 25 October 2026 Torchlit, 16×16, with its hero drawn at 32×32, was chosen as the game's art; the looks set aside are in the archives at the bottom.
