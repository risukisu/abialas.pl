New · 17 November 2026 · concepts, not in the game

The Title screen

The first screen of the game, before the Roster: a lone hero at a campfire under the night sky, the monsters waiting where the light ends, the title, and the menu. Three concepts, and A2, a second round of the first: the City's castle high on its crag, new trees, a hero who breathes. None of it is in the game yet. Each one is live: click it, walk the menu with the arrow keys, and Enter or a click says what a row opens.

Every concept carries the same menu, in the game's own words: Continue (shown once a character exists), New character, Roster, Settings and Quit (in the desktop app only), and the records every character adds to: the Compendium, the Graveyard, the Hall of Fame, and the Credits. Where those sit is what the concepts try: a panel and a row of icons (A and A2), things in the world (B), or words in the dark (C).
