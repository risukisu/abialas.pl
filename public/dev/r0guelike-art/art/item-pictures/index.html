Item pictures

Every item has two pictures: a large one on its card, when you look at it, and a small one where it lies on the floor. Each round of redrawing is kept here, newest first, with the pictures before and after it, so any round can be compared with what came before.

Round 1: The dark manner 18 November 2026

Every item redrawn darker and heavier: most of each piece sits in the low half of its colours, with a lit edge and a glint or two for light; shadows lean plum or cool, lights warm; blades are wider, limbs thicker, rings turned so the inside of the band shows; one or two made details a piece, a beaded setting, rim studs, a wrapped grip. Same items, same poses, same palette. The sword's and the rapier's gold runs from a yellow highlight down to bronze, and the other hilts vary from grey steel to bronze. The Ninth Grief and the clear pane keep their first pictures. Furia burns in its three states as before, on a darker blade. The pieces on the floor follow the cards: the same colours at their small size, the round shield and the rings a little bigger, the rings turned the same way; the garnet, sapphire, amethyst and diamond keep their first pieces on the floor.
