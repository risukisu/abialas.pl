The test room four ways

The same room drawn four ways and switched in place, to see where 32 by 32 pixels help: first only the figures, then the chest and the altar as well, then everything. Health bars are left out of all four, so only the drawing changes. Below it, telling things apart: a room of the Shallows with ideas for making the figures stand out.

Telling things apart 20 October 2026

Playing the Shallows in both Torchlits, the creatures were hard to pick out from the floor. Here a room of the Shallows is drawn as it is in play, then with ideas for making the figures stand out: a quieter world, more light, an outline round every figure, and a little movement. These are pictures of ideas, not the game; with each Torchlit, a crowd stands in the room, a spider right beside the hero.
