Telling things apart 20 October 2026

Playing the Shallows in both Torchlits, the creatures were hard to pick out from the floor. Here a room of the Shallows is drawn as it is in play, with ideas for making the figures stand out that can be turned on and off one by one, in any mix. These are pictures of ideas, not the game; with each Torchlit, a crowd stands in the room, a spider right beside the hero.

Feedback I had trouble telling elements apart, monsters didn't immediately pop out, it was in general hard to say what was going on in the game world
Torchlit
Turn on
  • Less colour: the walls and floors a little less colourful and darker.
  • More light: the light round the hero reaches further, and the dark beyond it is less deep.
  • A darker world: the walls and floors dimmed by a third while the figures and the things on the floor keep their own light, so the creatures stand above the ground; the floor's pattern stays as drawn. Proposed in place of a quieter floor: alone it helps most of the creatures, but a creature as dark as the spider needs brighter figures or outlines with it.
  • Brighter figures: the figures and the things on the floor never dimmed by the dark, a touch brighter and more colourful.
  • Outlines: a one-pixel outline round every figure, dark red for the creatures and white for the hero. A friendly figure would have green; none stands in this room.
  • Idle movement: every other figure dips its head by one pixel and back, a stand-in for the idle movement each figure would have.
