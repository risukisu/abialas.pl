Version 2.1 18 October 2026

Lava and water now meet floor, rock, walls and each other on edges that wander across the tiles: jagged with a black crust for lava, a soft wet shore for water. They do so in the test room, the Shallows and the Mines, and in a new room of their own, whose tiles are the pieces a game would keep in its tile atlas. Four more rooms show five ways of drawing the characters, every creature in the game so far, and chests: three plain designs shut, locked, open and open and empty, then wooden, iron-bound, gilded and crystal chests after Shattered Pixel Dungeon's, and a mimic. The City is as it was.

Where lava and water meet the rest

Lava and water against floor, rock, walls, moss and each other, on edges that wander across the tiles instead of following them: a stone hall on the left, a cave on the right. The tiles below are not cells but the pieces the edges are made of, which a game would keep in its tile atlas. Each piece sits where four tiles meet and is picked by which of the four are lava or water: sixteen shapes for each, every shape in four variants so an edge never repeats.

Version 2 18 October 2026

The walls are redrawn as uneven stone: stones of many sizes packed course by course, joints that run on from tile to tile, rough stone tops, pillars and corners built rather than cut. And the look now visits the game's three areas: the City at night, the halls of the Shallows, and the timbered galleries and caves of the Mines, each with its own tiles.

Try a bit more seamless walls joints, and walls irregular, with more variety in what's inside. Build 3 biomes we have so far, city, shallows and caves that will show tiles and sample rooms per each biome.
