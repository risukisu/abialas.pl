Torchlit, 16×16

Still being tried · 2 versions, newest first

Stone, earth and moss that flow across the tiles, walls of uneven stone, and light that falls off tile by tile around each torch. The figures stay small and bright. Since version 2 it is drawn in each of the game's areas: the City, the Shallows and the Mines. The tiles are shown unlit; the light is laid over the room as a whole.

Version 2 · 18 October 2026 Version 1 · 18 October 2026

Version 2 18 October 2026

The walls are redrawn as uneven stone: stones of many sizes packed course by course, joints that run on from tile to tile, rough stone tops, pillars and corners built rather than cut. And the look now visits the game's three areas: the City at night, the halls of the Shallows, and the timbered galleries and caves of the Mines, each with its own tiles.

Feedback Make the walls more varying, they all look like perfect squares and it looks like it's been made with Minecraft blocks. Try a bit more seamless walls joints, and walls irregular, with more variety in what's inside. Build 3 biomes we have so far, city, shallows and caves that will show tiles and sample rooms per each biome.
