Old Legends Grim Awakening

A single-player, turn-based, tile-based roguelike with a classic ADOM feel and modern quality-of-life.

Made by one person, risu. It runs in the browser as a playtest build; a desktop build is planned.

What's new
What it is

A city, and a long way down.

You make a character in the City of Angband and choose how far below it you go. One move, one turn. Every action saves.

Go down when you are ready.

The story is down there, under the City. Nothing forces you to go and get it. The stairs wait.

Freedom, and consequence.

Skip the story and just live in the world: fight, level up, grind. When the Island and its Towns come, anyone there will be killable, and the world will answer for it.

Strange from the first hour.

Strange things turn up everywhere, from the start, and no one explains them. You understand more the further down you get.

What you learn outlives you.

Characters die; what you learned does not. The game keeps its own record: a Chronicle for every character, a Graveyard for the permanently dead, a Hall of Fame still waiting for its first name.

The descent

One way down.

Every new character starts in the City. Below it the floors get older, darker and harder to explain. Scroll down; the candle goes with you.

Where every character starts

The City of Angband

Your first steps, your way back after a death in Adventurer Mode, and the one place that stays as it is. On the floors below, something may arrive out of your sight while you are there; the City stays as it is.

The Shallows

Four floors, made the first time you walk into them and kept for that character's life. No corridor ends in a bare wall. Each floor has a Special Room:

  • a vault behind a locked door
  • a pillared hall
  • a flooded chamber
  • a collapsed room
  • a round shrine
  • the miners' mess halls, bunk rooms and storerooms

The Mines

Eight floors below the Shallows, with a look of their own: rough rock, packed earth and timber. Galleries two abreast and rail-laid cross-cuts, timbered near the top and bare rock lower down, with working faces where the shift stopped. The Mines have rooms of their own:

  • a collapsed gallery
  • a flooded shaft
  • an ore face
  • a goblin warren

By the seventh floor the timber is gone: uneven tunnels, stalagmites and black pools. At the bottom waits the goblin king in his hall, the first boss. How that goes is yours to find out.

The end of the Early Game

The Sealed Door on the road

Shut. No one in the City can say who shut it, or why. Opening it wins the game at 1.0, and the Hall of Fame is waiting for the first character who does.

Death

Death is your choice.

You pick a Mode when you make a character. It decides what dying costs.

Adventurer

Die, lose gold, wake in the City

Death costs you gold and the walk back down. The character carries on.

Roguelike

Death is final

When the character dies, it stays dead and its save is deleted. Its name goes to the Graveyard.

Classic Roguelike

Final, and the Compendium sealed

Everything a Roguelike is, with the Compendium sealed.
